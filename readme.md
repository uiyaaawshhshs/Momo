## 🌌 About

> *My Love Mine All Mine* is a gentle and intimate song
> about love, mortality, and the desire for something
> beautiful to remain after we are gone.

The song uses the moon as a quiet symbol of permanence,
while the narrator reflects on what truly belongs to them.
